Frequently Asked Questions

Is Saudi ERP certified by ZATCA?

Yes. Our system is fully compliant with ZATCA Phase 1 (invoice generation) and Phase 2 (clearance and reporting) requirements. All invoices are generated in UBL 2.1 XML format with TLV-encoded QR codes as per ZATCA specifications.

هل النظام معتمد من هيئة الزكاة والضريبة والجمارك؟

نعم. يستوفي نظامنا متطلبات المرحلة الأولى والثانية من منظومة الفوترة الإلكترونية لهيئة زاتكا.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades at the next billing cycle.

Where is the data stored?

All data is stored on servers within Saudi Arabia for compliance with Saudi data residency requirements.

What Saudi payment methods are accepted for subscription?

We accept Mada, Visa/Mastercard, bank transfer (SADAD), and IBAN bank transfer. All transactions in SAR.

Does it support multiple companies/branches?

Yes. The Professional and Enterprise plans support multi-company setup with separate accounting per entity and consolidated reporting.
